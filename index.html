Desktop: Arrows = Move | Up = Jump | Down = Crouch (double-tap to lay) | Left-click = Shoot (aim with mouse) | Shift = Reload

Mobile: Drag the left joystick to move, jump or crouch. Drag the right joystick to aim and hold it down to fire. Ammo reloads automatically when empty.

Watch for red birds - shooting one enrages it and it attacks! Shoot it again to finish it off. Each hit costs 10 health. Past 6000 points, some red birds come after you right away.

Purple birds drop a power-up when shot twice (protective bubble, triple ammo or rapid fire). Walk into it to activate it. It lasts 30s, or disappears after 20s if not picked up.

Every 1500 points, a power-up falls from the sky: a full health refill, +10 ammo, or +20 max health (permanent). Walk into it to grab it.

Past 3000 points, geese show up at ground level - jump over walking geese and duck (crouch or lay) under low-flying ones, or lose 10 health. You can also just shoot one down in 2 hits.

Your gun automatically upgrades every 1500 points, rotating between faster fire rate, faster bullets, and an extra bullet per shot.

Play online with a friend: one of you hosts and shares the 5-letter code, the other joins with it. You each keep your own health/ammo/score in the same shared world.
